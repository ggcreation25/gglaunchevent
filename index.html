Our Core Engineering Manifesto

We don't build hollow decorative plastic models. Every miniature vehicle that leaves our workbench is built from raw 1.5mm cold-rolled steel, engineered with custom brass gearboxes, realistic steering columns, and genuine fluid-powered hydraulic lifting rams.

Zero Plastic Gears: Only hand-fitted brass pinions with high-viscosity grease that won't strip under load.
Mud Stress-Testing: Pulled through wet farm clay and real soil with 5KG+ payload tests before final approval.
Open-Source "If-Bill": Every nut, bolt, motor, and steel plate cost is published openly to teach the community.

STEP-BY-STEP MASTERY

The Universal Build Framework

Every long-form YouTube build follows our rigorous 6-stage engineering and testing discipline.

01

The Mud Pull Hook

Every build starts with a real physical challenge: Will this miniature tractor pull 5KG of pure farm soil, or will the gears snap?

02

Blueprint & Scale

Measuring real tractor wheelbases, calculating gear reduction ratios, and drafting millimeter-accurate cut templates on sheet metal.

03

Chassis & Metalwork

Cutting 1.5mm cold-rolled steel with angle grinders, drill press alignments, filing, and arc-welding rigid chassis box sections.

04

Powertrain & Gearbox

Fitting 775 high-torque DC motors, hand-lathing brass reduction gears, soldering electronic speed controls, and applying SV grease.

05

Automotive Enamel Finish

Multi-coat primer, color-matched Mahindra Red or JD Green enamel spray, authentic decals, and front grille detailing.

06

Mud Test & The If-Bill

Extreme outdoor field trials in red mud, followed by publishing the 100% itemized component cost table for our audience.
